About Us

Sweeping Beauty Cleaning Services is a Canadian cleaning company based in Summerland, British Columbia. Corrina Herman is the owner/operator of the business. She is 'Sweeping Beauty' with 30-plus years experience in the business. Sweeping Beauty Cleaning Services is insured and WorkSafe BC Accredited. We use environmentally friendly products so all of our services are safe for the whole family and your pets.
